# Ferdi story bank

Use this reference as private creative context for Ideas by Ferdi. These facts may inspire or deepen a story, but they are not a checklist and must never be inserted just to make a video seem profound.

## Core message

Ferdi wants to show how people can keep moving when life is not going well, regain energy after getting stuck, and do something useful for their physical and mental health. Sport is his recurring vehicle for agency, health, and long-term consistency—not a promise that training fixes every problem.

The tone should be positive without pretending that motivation is constant. His recurring action is: struggle, notice it honestly, choose a next step, and try again.

## Audience and value

- Students and pupils who feel too drained after studying or school to start.
- Working adults who struggle to move after work.
- People with full ordinary lives, not full-time fitness creators.
- Viewers should receive realistic examples of combining health, sport, work, and study without needing a perfect routine.

The desired response is not “Ferdi is more disciplined than me.” It is “He also struggles, so maybe I can take one step too.”

## Distinctive perspective

For many fitness creators, sport supposedly created discipline in every other area. Ferdi's direction was partly reversed: learning to face difficult things elsewhere transferred into sport, while years of gym and endurance training then taught him to stay with long processes.

He repeatedly chooses challenges he is not naturally prepared for. That can be a useful identity thread, but do not call him fearless or exceptional.

## School and education

- Ferdi had recurring grades around 3–5, especially in mathematics, computer science, physics, German, and Latin.
- In one period he had roughly three subjects around 4.5–4.6 and came close to failing.
- Parent-teacher meetings caused real fear and shame. He sometimes hid in his room afterward and cried. His parents were not cruel; the fear came largely from knowing he was doing badly and disappointing himself.
- Toward the Abitur he gradually understood that studying was something he did for his own future. Maturing and beginning despite resistance mattered more than one dramatic epiphany.
- He took a gap year because he did not know what he wanted to do.
- He later chose Wirtschaftsinformatik at TUM partly because it was a difficult challenge close to subjects he had previously struggled with.
- In the first semester he failed the first exam and was unsure whether he would pass the second because he lacked prior computer-science knowledge. He continued through the exam period, performed better in other modules, and stayed.

Safe framing: “vom 5er-Schüler zum TUM-Studenten” may be used when relevant. Do not upgrade this to “Top-Student,” genius, or proof that anyone can achieve anything.

## Earlier social media

- At age 13–14 Ferdi already created social content and reached about 45,000 followers.
- Schoolmates called things through the hallway and watched his videos in the classroom. This affected him and contributed to closing himself off.
- He later stopped pursuing something he genuinely enjoyed and tried to prove that he could do more technical or conventionally respectable work.
- Returning to social media now means overcoming embarrassment and choosing what he actually likes again.

Do not describe this as extreme or relentless bullying. Use measured language such as being mocked, exposed, or made uncomfortable. Do not name classmates or other private people.

## Body, training, and health

- Ferdi was mocked or felt insecure about visible birthmarks, his ears, and his body.
- He has experienced scoliosis and a rounded back. Do not claim that training cured either condition or make medical recommendations.
- He remains dissatisfied with parts of his appearance but has learned to value and love what his body can do and how healthy and functional it is.
- Reaching a 100 kg bench press was a meaningful personal milestone after previously failing it.
- He has lost more than 10 kg on multiple occasions. Use exact amounts only when Ferdi confirms them for the specific story.
- His current tension is wanting to become stronger and broader while enjoying longer running. He sometimes worries that endurance work harms muscle progress and questions whether his hybrid plan makes sense.
- Knee tolerance can limit running. Do not diagnose the knee or prescribe treatment.

## Current routine and goals

Time-sensitive snapshot from September 2026; confirm before using as a current fact:

- TUM Wirtschaftsinformatik.
- During semester breaks: roughly 20–40 work hours per week; during semester: around 20.
- Gym at least four times weekly, often Push / Pull / Legs / Upper.
- Running two to three times weekly when the knee allows.
- Recently began swimming and wants roughly one session weekly.
- Preparing for his first-ever official half marathon across the Golden Gate Bridge in San Francisco, roughly seven weeks after signing up, and considering a longer YouTube video about it.
- He discovered the race spontaneously while sitting on a train. It falls exactly within the roughly two weeks he expects to be in the United States, so he impulsively paid the entry fee.
- Current target for the half marathon: under two hours. Confirm the target and remaining weeks before using them later.
- Intended running structure: one interval session, one easy run, and one progressively longer long run per week while keeping four weekly gym sessions.
- Main sporting tension: become stronger while building endurance rather than choosing only one identity.
- He would currently also like to lower his body-fat level and feel more satisfied with his physique. Longer runs can feel as if they are costing him gym progress, even though he is excited about the race and grateful to be healthy enough to attempt it.

Never stack all of these numbers into an opening unless the video is explicitly testing the feasibility of the schedule.

## Doomscrolling and imperfect consistency

- Ferdi can scroll in the morning, around midday, late at night, after work, or before the gym.
- A session can cost one to two hours. He then becomes angry and disappointed with himself and may let that mood damage the next part of the day.
- This is not a fake “I woke up five minutes late” flaw. It is a recurring example of losing agency and trying to regain it.

Useful arc: scrolling → self-directed anger → notice that anger is wasting even more time → take one small physical action → mood may improve, but the habit is not magically solved.

## Alcohol and rigidity

- From age 15 to 17 Ferdi drank no alcohol because he imagined becoming an extremely disciplined “Sigma/Alpha” gym person.
- He did not reach every goal, including the 100 kg bench at that time, and later felt that extreme rigidity cost him parts of normal teenage life.
- Present lesson: enjoyment and imperfect choices can coexist with ambition; optimal is not always a good life.

Do not frame abstinence as bad, drinking as required, or Ferdi's choice as a moral lesson for others. The story is about rigidity and balance.

## Food and humor bank

- Often eats one or two kiwis daily.
- Likes inexpensive supermarket fruit and is willing to turn repetitive or unusual consumption into a playful measurable premise.
- Potential food lens: protein per euro, calories or fuel per euro, practicality for students, taste, enjoyment, and usefulness around training.
- Ice cream or other non-optimal food may appear as enjoyable fuel rather than a guilt confession.
- “Over/Under” and odd-food challenges should be original; use placeholders when the actual item and quantity are not known.

Food content does not need school trauma or TUM context. A running counter, price constraint, taste test, or performance question can provide enough story engine.

## Relevance gate

Use a personal fact only when at least one is true:

1. It explains why today's event matters to Ferdi.
2. It changes the stakes or expected result.
3. It creates a precise connection to the viewer's struggle.
4. The user explicitly asks to tell that part of his history.

For ordinary lifestyle or humor videos, use at most one deeper callback. Deep seated stories may combine several facts when they form one causal arc.

## Sensitive framing rules

- Anonymize everyone except Ferdi unless he explicitly authorizes a name.
- Do not vilify his parents, teachers, classmates, partner, or family.
- Do not intensify “mocked and hurt” into severe abuse or extreme bullying.
- Never invent dialogue, diagnoses, motives, tears, or exact grades/dates.
- Do not use shame about appearance, food, alcohol, grades, or scrolling as the CTA.
- Treat impressive outcomes as moments in an unfinished process.
