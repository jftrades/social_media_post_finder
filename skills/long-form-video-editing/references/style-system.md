# Ferdi long-form style system

## Typography

- **Alte Haas Grotesk Bold:** default for titles, explanations, numbers, selective spoken emphasis, labels, and thumbnail copy. Use white by default with a soft blurred black shadow.
- **Child Hood:** personal note, thought, correction, diary annotation, arrow label, or imperfect handwritten aside. Do not use for full paragraphs.
- **Race Day:** one- or two-word impact heading. Keep it rare enough to retain force.

Text is selective, not full-time karaoke. Its job is to clarify a number, rule, choice, punchline, progress marker, or change in stakes.

## Camera color

Ask at Level 2. If approved, every confirmed camera file receives:

1. `CINELIKE D to REC 709_26.P1003055.cube`, mixed to 80%.
2. `MERRY_MEN_II.cube`, applied after the first mix and mixed to 30%.

Never apply these LUTs to named phone clips, screen recordings, external exports, graphics, or already graded footage. A project-level “all camera” answer enables them for all clips unless a segment overrides `camera: false`.

## Zooms and motion

- Use a smooth zoom for a reaction, important claim, joke, result, or newly revealed problem.
- Reset on a natural cut or after the emphasized beat.
- Prefer one motivated move over constant micro-motion.
- For a training montage, motion may follow impact, repetition, or music rhythm, but must not make technique unreadable.

## SFX

Choose by semantic job: impact, transition, selection/UI, comic failure, crowd reaction, charge/build, shutdown, or tactile action. Document the match in the job. SFX belong in `02_animated.mp4` and `03_final.mp4`, never `01_cut.mp4`.

## Music and tension

Map music to story function before selecting files:

- curiosity/cold open;
- orientation or light routine;
- forward progress;
- uncertainty/setback;
- escalation/build;
- decisive attempt;
- payoff/release;
- reflection/outro.

Use multiple tracks when functions change. Prefer an intentional cut, short bridge, riser, impact, or crossfade at a story turn. Duck music under dialogue and let it rise only where the spoken content permits. Do not use copyrighted library files unless Ferdi has the right to publish them.

## Thumbnail

Use a real project frame or still with a clear human state, action, scale, or contrast. The title and thumbnail must promise the same video. Default text uses Alte Haas; Race Day may add one short accent. Avoid clutter and unprovable claims.
