# Long-form intake

Run the levels in order. Ask only unanswered items. Wait for Ferdi after each level; do not collapse all three into one message.

## Level 1 — Video and promise

Ask together:

1. Which folder under `long_form_unfinished_projects` is the project?
2. What is the video's working title, central viewer promise/open question, target viewer, and desired duration?
3. Is there a script, voice-over, rough outline, required chronology, or known result?
4. Which reference video is primary, which may be secondary, and what specifically felt entertaining about them? If Ferdi does not choose, offer the closest analyzed references by story type.
5. Confirm delivery settings. Default only when accepted: 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 MP4, 256 kbps AAC.

Summarize the chosen promise, result withheld from the opening, target length, and reference blend. Wait.

## Level 2 — Visual and audio system

Ask together:

1. Are all source videos camera footage? If not, which named files are phone, screen recording, external, or already graded?
2. Apply the two camera LUTs? State the exact default: CINELIKE D to REC 709 at 80%, then MERRY_MEN II at 30%, only on confirmed camera files.
3. Should the edit use selective emphasis text only, recurring training data, chapter cards, handwritten notes, or another named overlay type? Alte Haas is the default; Child Hood is for notes; Race Day is for one- or two-word impact headings.
4. May the editor choose freely from all `background_music` and `sound_fx`, or are any files forbidden? Multiple music cues are the default.
5. Should speech be leveled for consistent intelligibility and should original location sound remain under montages?
6. Confirm thumbnail wording or allow the editor to propose the clearest footage-backed wording.

Summarize visual rules, camera exclusions, audio permissions, and thumbnail direction. Wait.

## Level 3 — Files, structure, and exceptions

Inventory the project first, then ask together:

1. Confirm each file's role: main speaking take, live scene, training clip, B-roll, voice-over, external asset, or unusable take.
2. What must stay, what must be removed, and which chronology may not be changed?
3. May clips be shortened, reordered, cropped, stabilized, or speed-ramped? Name exceptions.
4. May long-form library B-roll be used, and if so which files or topics are allowed?
5. Confirm pronunciation/name spellings, sensitive material, injury/safety context, and any claims that need on-screen qualification.
6. Confirm one-shot delivery of `01_cut.mp4`, `02_animated.mp4`, `03_final.mp4`, and `thumbnail.jpg` with no intermediate approval.

Write the answers into `job.json`, mark all three levels `confirmed: true`, then proceed autonomously.
