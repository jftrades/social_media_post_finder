# Transferable Rules — Relationship-Led Training Vlog

- Identify the relationship and episode goal immediately, then enter a live scene.
- Treat reactions and conversational timing as primary footage; do not cover every line with B-roll.
- Use short camera-facing bridges whenever time/location changes would otherwise feel random.
- Give each training scene a local question: pace target, technique test, mini-game, comparison, or result.
- Keep social or food scenes only when they reveal the relationship or pay off the video's promise.
- Use data overlays as recurring proof and comedy motifs, not as decoration.
- Let music bridge or montage; drop it under the best exchanges.
- Ferdi adaptation: combine this with the challenge reference if a stronger episode-level escalation is needed.
