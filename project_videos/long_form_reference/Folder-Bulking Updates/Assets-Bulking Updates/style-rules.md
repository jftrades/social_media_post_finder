# Transferable Rules — Lifestyle / Bulking Update

- Choose this pace only when personality, training atmosphere, or social chemistry is the main product.
- Let a heavy attempt play through setup, effort, and reaction; do not cut every rep into a micro-montage.
- Retain silence when it improves banter or lets a failed attempt land. Remove repetition from pure exposition.
- Use a hard chapter reset before a long direct update so the viewer understands the mode change.
- Do not bury the actual update behind fourteen minutes of montage unless the footage itself is the promised entertainment.
- Prefer one strong music-led block over constant background music. Change or remove the track at the direct-address reset.
- Ferdi adaptation: mix this reference with a clearer goal-based reference when the video needs a measurable payoff.
