# Long-Form Reference Style Index

These packs describe reusable editing mechanisms, not templates to copy. Scene-change counts are detector estimates from final exports; they are useful for comparing pacing, but they are not exact editorial cut counts. Raw-footage pauses that were removed cannot be measured from the export.

## Selection rules

- For a measurable endurance challenge, start with **I Tried Cycling 100 Miles**; borrow its visible goal, progress markers, field updates, and earned payoff.
- For a multi-day training diary, start with **A Week Of Triathlon Training**; borrow its alternation between action, reflection, and measurable proof.
- For a slower gym/lifestyle update, start with **Bulking Updates**; borrow its willingness to let training sequences breathe and its clear late reset into direct explanation.
- For a video whose entertainment comes from another person, start with **Training my Little Sister**; protect live dialogue, reactions, and relationship beats instead of over-animating them.
- Combine at most two primary references for one edit. State the mechanism taken from each; never copy titles, jokes, brand graphics, or exact sequences.

Each `Folder-…/Assets-…/` pack contains `analysis.md`, `style-rules.md`, transcripts, detector data, overview sheets, timestamped evidence stills, transition strips, and audio visualizations.

Windows exception: the Cycling filename stem ends in three periods. Its analysis pack uses the visually equivalent final ellipsis `…`; folders ending in a literal period cannot be addressed reliably by Win32 or Git.
