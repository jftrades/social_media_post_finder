# Transferable Rules — Measurable Endurance Challenge

- Establish baseline, target, and 2–3 success conditions in the opening 30 seconds.
- Turn preparation into visible objects and decisions: food, gear, route, weather, start line.
- Re-hook with milestones only when they alter the forecast: early pace, halfway benchmark, hard climb, missing fuel, final ten miles.
- Use maps, mileage, heart rate, and nutrition as proof; keep them short and readable.
- Cut routine riding quickly, then hold longer on fear, pain, relief, or a consequential conversation.
- Use zoom-ins on genuine reaction/reveal beats. Avoid pulsing zooms on every sentence.
- Use SFX for UI/data reveals, major numbers, and chapter impacts—not under every B-roll cut.
- Score distinct emotional functions with different cues; remove or duck music when live distress is stronger.
- The final frame sequence must visibly prove the target and then answer what the challenge changed.
