# Analysis — I Tried Cycling 100 Miles

## What the edit is doing

This 11:36 challenge video has the clearest narrative spine in the set. It establishes three goals, exposes under-preparation, then uses mileage, hills, fuel, water, pain, and emotion as a ladder of escalating constraints. The number 100 supplies a built-in ending; the edit keeps showing evidence so the payoff feels earned.

**Observed:** timestamps, transcript content, visible milestone/data overlays, scene-change estimates, retained silences, and the on-screen finish. **Inferred:** the viewer-prediction curve and emotional function of music sections. Exact SFX, J/L cuts, speed ramps, and raw pauses require direct source verification.
