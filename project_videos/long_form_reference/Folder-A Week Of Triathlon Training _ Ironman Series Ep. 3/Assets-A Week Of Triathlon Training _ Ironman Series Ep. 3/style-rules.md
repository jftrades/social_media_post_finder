# Transferable Rules — Training Week Diary

- Open with target, time horizon, and this episode's specific problem within roughly 20 seconds.
- Use a repeated loop: plan → training proof → immediate reaction → what changed.
- Remove dead air from explanations, but preserve breaths, effort, or hesitation when they prove difficulty.
- Use metrics, app screens, and training-plan text as evidence. Do not cover action with decorative overlays.
- Let vulnerable or body-image passages run at lower visual density than workout montages.
- Re-state the stakes at the midpoint when multiple days or locations make the story diffuse.
- Build music in chapters: intent, friction, comeback, reflection, final forward motion. Duck or remove it for the most candid sentences.
- Ferdi adaptation: Alte Haas Grotesk Bold for plan/metric text, Child Hood for personal note annotations, Race Day only for one- or two-word chapter impacts.
